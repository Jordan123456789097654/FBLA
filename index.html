Who We Are

About Our Chapter

🎯

Our Mission

FBLA-Middle Level inspires and prepares students to become community-minded business leaders in a global society through relevant career preparation and leadership experiences.

🏆

Competitions

Members compete at regional, state, and national levels in events like business communication, computer problem solving, digital citizenship, and more.

🤝

Community Service

We believe in giving back. Our chapter participates in community service projects throughout the year, making a positive impact in our local community.

📈

Leadership

Through workshops, conferences, and hands-on projects, members develop critical leadership skills that will serve them throughout their academic and professional careers.
