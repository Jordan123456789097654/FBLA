About FBLA

The Future Business Leaders of America Middle Level (FBLA-ML) at Little Mill Middle School helps students transition into the business world, developing leadership, communication, and teamwork skills.

Mission

To bring business and education together in a positive working relationship through innovative leadership and career development programs.

Competitions

Test your business knowledge and skills in various competitive events at the regional, state, and national levels.

Community Service

Give back to the community through organized service projects and volunteer opportunities.

Leadership

Develop essential leadership skills through officer positions, committee roles, and leadership conferences.

FBLA Creed

I believe education is the right of every person.

I believe the future depends on mutual understanding and cooperation among business, industry, labor, religious, family, and educational institutions, as well as people around the world.

I agree to do my best to develop my abilities and to use them for the improvement of my home, business, community, and the world.

FBLA Pledge

I solemnly promise to uphold the aims and responsibilities of FBLA-PBL and, as an active member, I shall strive to develop the qualities necessary in becoming a responsible business leader.
